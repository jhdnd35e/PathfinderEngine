## Rules licenses

Pathfinder Second Edition's rules corpus spans more than one licensing generation.
Paizo's current licensing guidance distinguishes game-rules content released under
the Open RPG Creative (ORC) License from legacy game-rules content released under
the Open Game License (OGL). Those licenses do not automatically license Paizo's
Reserved Material/Product Identity.

References:
- https://paizo.com/licenses
- https://paizo.com/licenses/communityuse
- https://paizo.com/orclicense

Generated records therefore preserve upstream source, release date, URL, and
Legacy/Remaster relationships instead of treating the whole Archives of Nethys
index as one uniformly licensed body of material.

The presence of a field in the Archives of Nethys search index does not by itself
establish that the field is ORC Licensed Material or OGL Open Game Content.
Downstream publication or commercial use must apply the licenses and notices of the
underlying source material and should not assume that setting lore, proper nouns,
art, or other Reserved Material/Product Identity is available under a rules license.
